01The question

What makes good software?

Today anyone can build a tool in an afternoon, and that freedom is a good thing. Turning it into something a business can depend on takes four things.

  1. 01

    Safe

    We know where the data centre is and who owns the data. Every action leaves a trace.

    • Encrypted database
    • Row-level security
    • Audit trail on every action
  2. 02

    Scalable

    Good architecture on good infrastructure gives performance. When more people use the system, it keeps running smoothly.

    • Google Cloud infrastructure
    • Inside your own cloud project
    • Highly available and backed up
  3. 03

    Stable

    Data stored in an organised way that your people and your AI agents can reach. Without that, nothing gets automated.

    • One source of truth
    • Open to AI agents through MCP
    • Governance over what agents do
  4. 04

    Compliant

    Validated, tested and released through automated deployments, every day if needed.

    • Automated tests and CI/CD
    • Validation evidence when required
    • Privacy terms in writing
