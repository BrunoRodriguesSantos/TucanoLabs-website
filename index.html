Meet Kea · Your AI Teammate

An AI assistant that works like an employee.

Kea is the LLM-based AI at the heart of Tucano Nest. It's not a chatbot bolted onto the side. Kea has secure access to your business context, so it can actually get things done: chase overdue invoices, prep a sales report before your Monday meeting, or spot the stock line that's about to run out.

  • Ask anything about your business in plain English and get real answers.
  • Delegates real work: drafting, reconciling, reporting and following up.
  • Learns your business context across ERP, CRM and BI, because it lives in all three.
  • You stay in control: Kea proposes, you approve, it executes.

About Us

A founder-built startup, shipping our own product.

Tucano Labs is a New Zealand tech startup founded by two engineers with a combined 30+ years building software and data platforms for global companies. We saw small and mid-sized businesses drowning in disconnected tools, so we're building the platform we always wished existed: one AI-powered system that runs the whole business.

  • Product-led: we design, build and operate Tucano Nest ourselves.
  • AI-native from day one: Kea is core to the product, not an add-on.
  • Cloud-native and digital-first: built on Google Cloud, delivered as SaaS.
