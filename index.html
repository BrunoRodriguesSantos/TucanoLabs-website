Our Product · Tucano Nest

ERP. CRM. BI. AI. One nest for everything.

Tucano Nest is the AI-powered, cloud-based one-stop shop for running a business. Instead of stitching together five different tools, your finance, sales, operations and analytics live in a single system that learns how your business works.

  1. 01 ERP Finance & Operations
  2. 02 CRM Sales & Customers
  3. 03 BI Insights & Reporting
  4. 04 Kea AI That Does the Work

ERP · Finance & Operations

Invoicing, purchasing, inventory and cash flow in one place, with AI keeping the books tidy and flagging what needs attention.

  • Invoicing & payments tracking
  • Inventory & purchasing
  • AI-assisted reconciliation & alerts

CRM · Sales & Customers

A pipeline that updates itself. Contacts, deals and quotes stay in sync with your invoices and stock, because it's all one system.

  • Pipeline & deal management
  • Contacts, quotes & follow-ups
  • AI-drafted emails & next-step suggestions

BI · Insights & Reporting

Real-time dashboards and reports built from your live business data. Ask a question in plain English and get the chart, not a data export.

  • Live dashboards & KPIs
  • Natural-language reporting via Kea
  • Forecasting & trend detection

Kea · AI That Does the Work

Kea is an LLM-based assistant that works like a member of your team: answering questions, drafting documents and running routine workflows.

  • Plain-English questions over your data
  • Automated routine workflows
  • Drafts invoices, emails & reports

Meet Kea · Your AI Teammate

An AI assistant that works like an employee.

Kea is the LLM-based AI at the heart of Tucano Nest. It's not a chatbot bolted onto the side. Kea has secure access to your business context, so it can actually get things done: chase overdue invoices, prep a sales report before your Monday meeting, or spot the stock line that's about to run out.

  • Ask anything about your business in plain English and get real answers.
  • Delegates real work: drafting, reconciling, reporting and following up.
  • Learns your business context across ERP, CRM and BI, because it lives in all three.
  • You stay in control: Kea proposes, you approve, it executes.

About Us

A founder-built startup, shipping our own product.

Tucano Labs is a New Zealand tech startup founded by two engineers with a combined 30+ years building software and data platforms for global companies. We saw small and mid-sized businesses drowning in disconnected tools, so we're building the platform we always wished existed: one AI-powered system that runs the whole business.

  • Product-led: we design, build and operate Tucano Nest ourselves.
  • AI-native from day one: Kea is core to the product, not an add-on.
  • Cloud-native and digital-first: built on Google Cloud, delivered as SaaS.
